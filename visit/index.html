Getting Here

Directions & transport

However you are travelling, the campus is straightforward to reach from anywhere in Ganta City.

By road from Ganta City centre

From the Ganta City centre, take the main road towards the Jacob Town Community. The campus is on the right, marked by its yellow wall, black gate, and the Panther Kingdom mural.

By public transport

Commercial taxis and motorbike taxis (pen-pen) run regularly between the Ganta City centre and Jacob Town Community. Ask the driver for the “AME Zion University campus”.

From Saclepea or Sanniquellie

Travel to Ganta City on the Saclepea–Ganta highway, then follow the directions above into the Jacob Town Community. The campus is a short distance from the main highway.

Office hours

The Admissions & Records Office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Visitors are welcome during these hours; no appointment is required.
