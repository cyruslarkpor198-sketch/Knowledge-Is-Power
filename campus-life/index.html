Campus Life

Where Panthers grow

Study, sport, worship, and service — the four rhythms of student life at the Nimba Extension Campus.

Student Life

A campus that keeps you moving

From the computer laboratory to the reading room, from the sports field to the chapel, there is always somewhere to be and something to do.

Students at the Nimba Extension Campus come from every district of Nimba County and beyond. Classes are small enough that lecturers know your name, and the campus community is close enough that friendships formed here last well beyond graduation.

The campus is a short distance from the Ganta City centre, so students can combine study with work, family responsibilities, and community life — without leaving the county.

Athletics

The Panthers on the pitch

Our football team carries the university name — and the Nimba Extension badge — into competition across the county.

Sport is part of the education here. The Panthers train on the campus field and compete in county and inter-campus fixtures, giving students a shared identity and a reason to show up for one another.

Alongside football, students take part in kickball, athletics, and recreational sport throughout the academic year. Trials are open to every registered student.

Get Involved

Student organizations

Clubs and societies run by students, for students — and open to every registered member of the campus.

Student Government Association

The elected voice of the student body, representing student interests to campus administration and organising campus-wide activities.

Wesley Foundation & Chapel Choir

Weekly worship, Bible study, and the campus choir, carrying forward the Wesleyan heritage of the university.

Debate & Public Speaking Society

Competitive debate, oratory, and public speaking training for students across every college.

Agriculture & Community Service Club

Hands-on farming projects, outreach to surrounding communities, and practical food-security work.

Health & First Aid Corps

Health awareness campaigns, first aid training, and community health outreach in Ganta City and nearby towns.

Panthers Athletics

Football, kickball, and track teams that compete across Nimba County under the Panther banner.

Facilities

What is on campus

A compact, well-kept campus with the facilities students actually use every week.

Computer Laboratory

A networked computer lab with printing facilities, used for ICT courses, research, and student assignments.

Library & Reading Room

Reference collections, course texts, and quiet study space for students and faculty.

Lecture Halls & Classrooms

Spacious, well-ventilated classrooms and a covered pavilion used for assemblies and ceremonies.

Registrar & Records Office

On-campus registration, transcript processing, and academic record keeping for all students.

Sports Field

An open playing field for football, kickball, and physical education, and the home ground of the Panthers.

Campus Grounds & Gate

A secure, walled campus with a gated entrance, paved walkways, and landscaped grounds in Jacob Town Community.
