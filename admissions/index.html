How to Apply

Six steps to enrolment

Applications are handled by the Admissions & Records Office on campus. Follow the steps below in order and keep a copy of everything you submit.

01

Request an application

Email the Admissions Office at amezion.nec2024@gmail.com or visit the campus in Jacob Town Community, Ganta City, to collect an application form.

02

Complete the form

Fill in the application form in full and attach all required documents. Incomplete applications cannot be processed.

03

Submit your documents

Return the completed form and supporting documents to the Admissions & Records Office on campus, or send scanned copies by email.

04

Sit the entrance assessment

Applicants sit a short entrance assessment in English and mathematics. The Admissions Office will notify you of the date and venue.

05

Receive your admission letter

Successful applicants receive an official admission letter stating the college, programme, and reporting date.

06

Register and begin classes

Pay the required fees at the campus business office, complete registration, and join orientation with your new cohort.

Requirements

What you will need to submit

Bring originals for verification and one clear photocopy of each document for the campus file.

Undergraduate Applicants

  • Completed application form
  • High school diploma or WAEC/WASSCE certificate
  • Official high school transcript
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Letter of good conduct (where available)

Transfer & Graduate Applicants

  • Completed application form
  • Official transcripts from all previous institutions
  • Statement of purpose (one page)
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Evidence of good standing at your previous institution

Important Dates

The academic calendar at a glance

The campus runs two semesters each academic year. Exact dates are confirmed by the Registrar each term — always check with the office before you travel.
