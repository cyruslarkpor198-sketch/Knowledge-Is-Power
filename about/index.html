About Us

A tradition of learning since 1887

The Nimba Extension Campus carries the mission of the African Methodist Episcopal Zion University into the heart of Nimba County.

Our Story

From a mission school to a national university

AME Zion University traces its origins to 1887, when the African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church established a school in Liberia to educate the children of a young nation. What began as a small mission school grew into one of Liberia's most enduring institutions of higher learning — a university shaped by the Wesleyan conviction that education is both a right and a responsibility.

In 2024, the university extended its mission to Nimba County with the opening of the Nimba Extension Campus in the Jacob Town Community of Ganta City. The campus was established to bring degree programmes within reach of students who could not travel to Monrovia, and to support the region's growing demand for teachers, health workers, agribusiness leaders, and public servants.

Today the Nimba Extension Campus — known across the county as The Panther Kingdom — serves students from every district of Nimba and beyond, offering programmes across eight colleges under the leadership of the university's Board of Trustees.

Purpose

Vision & Mission

What we are building, and why it matters for Nimba County and for Liberia.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence in Liberia's north-central region, producing graduates of competence, character, and Christian conscience who transform their communities and their nation.

Our Mission

The Nimba Extension Campus of the African Methodist Episcopal Zion University exists to provide accessible, affordable, and high-quality higher education; to advance knowledge through teaching, research, and community service; and to nurture the intellectual, moral, and spiritual development of every student in the Wesleyan tradition.

What We Stand For

Our core values

Four commitments that shape how we teach, how we govern, and how we serve.

Faith

We are rooted in the Wesleyan tradition of the African Methodist Episcopal Zion Church, and we hold that learning and faith belong together.

Excellence

We set high academic standards and hold ourselves and our students to them, in the classroom and in the field.

Integrity

We teach and model honesty, accountability, and respect for the dignity of every person.

Service

We measure our success by the good our graduates do in Nimba County, in Liberia, and beyond.
