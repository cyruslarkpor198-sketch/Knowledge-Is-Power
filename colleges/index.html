How Study Works Here

Practical, local, and rigorous

Our academic model is built for students who intend to work in Liberia — and to lead there.

  • Programmes are taught on campus in Ganta City by resident and visiting faculty.
  • Each college combines classroom instruction with practical, field-based training.
  • Course outlines and credit requirements are available from the Registrar on request.
  • New intakes open at the start of each semester — see the Admissions page for dates.

Not sure which college is right for you?

The Admissions & Records Office can talk you through the entry requirements, the length of each programme, and the careers our graduates move into. There is no charge for the conversation.
