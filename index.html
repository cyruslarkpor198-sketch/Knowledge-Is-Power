Welcome to the Panther Kingdom

A university campus built for Nimba County

The Nimba Extension Campus brings the 130-year academic tradition of the African Methodist Episcopal Zion University to the heart of Nimba County.

Located in the Jacob Town Community of Ganta City, our campus offers affordable, high-quality degree programmes designed for the students, families, and communities of Liberia's north-central region — so that a university education no longer requires leaving home.

From criminal justice and business to agriculture, health sciences, and divinity, our eight colleges prepare graduates to serve with competence, character, and faith.

  • Degree programmes across eight colleges
  • On-campus computer laboratory
  • Affordable tuition and flexible entry
  • Rooted in the Wesleyan tradition
